# Ink

A fictional brand, for an app that keeps a person's attention on one thing: black ink on white
paper, white on black at night, and nothing else. No real brand is represented here.

## Look

- **Black and white only.** Every surface, text, outline, and the primary action are black,
  white, or a grey between them. The primary button is a black block with a white label; at night
  it turns white with a black label.
- **Red is kept for danger.** It is the only color, and it appears only on destructive actions
  and their warnings, so it is never mistaken for decoration.
- **Square.** Every corner is 0px. Controls read as cut from the page, not floating on it.
- **Quiet type.** Labels are set in Archivo at medium weight (500): white on black needs no more
  weight to be read. Archivo is under the SIL Open Font License and covers Vietnamese. Apps that
  do not bundle it fall back to the system font.
- **Night is black.** The dark modes put the canvas at pure black, so a phone on a desk at night
  shows almost no light.

## Voice

Short, plain, and in the second person. Say what will happen and when it ends; never praise,
never scold.

| Write                   | Not                                     |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Lock until 11:30        | Start your amazing focus session!       |
| Locked. Opens at 11:30. | Oops! You can't use this app right now. |
| End the session early   | Give up?                                |
| Delete this schedule    | Are you sure you want to delete?        |

## Usage

- One primary action per screen: it is the only black block on it.
- Use the danger variant only to end something early or delete something, and say what.
- Do not tint surfaces or text with a color of your own; the theme has none to offer.

## Accessibility

Every declared pair meets its contrast requirement in all four modes: 4.5:1 for text in the
standard modes, 7:1 in the high-contrast modes, and 3:1 for outlines and the focus ring.
