Color

Components use roles, never raw colors. A role such as color.fg.muted has a value in each of the four color modes, so a component styled with roles works in all of them.

This page shows the base brand: slate neutrals with a raincoat-yellow accent. A theme replaces the brand and neutral ramps and may remap any role.

Backgrounds

color.bg.canvas The page background. #ffffff #0a0e12 #ffffff #000000

color.bg.subtle A quiet fill for grouping content, such as a table header. #f1f4f7 #282e36 #f1f4f7 #0a0e12
color.bg.inverse A surface in the opposite scheme, such as a toast. #161b21 #f9fafc #000000 #ffffff
color.bg.disabled Fill of a disabled control. #f1f4f7 #282e36 #f1f4f7 #161b21
