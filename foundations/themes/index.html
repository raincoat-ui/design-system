2.6

Themes

A theme is a directory of overrides on top of the base token source. It may change the brand and neutral ramps, the color each role takes in each mode, font families and weights, radii, the shadows of raised surfaces and overlays, and the outline of a card. It cannot change spacing, sizes, touch targets, focus rules, or contrast minimums, and it cannot add or remove a token. Each theme is a package of its own on every platform, so an app installs only its own. To add one, see Add a theme.

The plates below use each theme’s own stylesheet, and the fonts it carries in themes/<id>/fonts/. The theme packages do not bundle fonts yet: an app that does not load a theme’s font falls back to the system font.

Base

Section titled “Base”

White paper, near-neutral ink, and raincoat yellow as the one signal; every corner 2px.

Platform Package
npm @raincoat-ui/theme-base
Maven io.github.raincoat-ui:theme-base
Swift RaincoatThemeBase, applied with .raincoatTheme(.base)

The base theme is the token source itself: every other theme starts from it. All 524 declared color pairs meet their contrast requirement in every mode.

Ink

Section titled “Ink”

Black and white only: black ink on white paper, white on black at night, red kept for danger, every corner square.

Platform Package
npm @raincoat-ui/theme-ink
Maven io.github.raincoat-ui:theme-ink
Swift RaincoatThemeInk, applied with .raincoatTheme(.ink)

It overrides 97 tokens, in themes/ink/. All 524 declared color pairs meet their contrast requirement in every mode.

A fictional brand, for an app that keeps a person’s attention on one thing: black ink on white paper, white on black at night, and nothing else. No real brand is represented here.

Look

Section titled “Look”
  • Black and white only. Every surface, text, outline, and the primary action are black, white, or a grey between them. The primary button is a black block with a white label; at night it turns white with a black label.
  • Red is kept for danger. It is the only color, and it appears only on destructive actions and their warnings, so it is never mistaken for decoration.
  • Square. Every corner is 0px. Controls read as cut from the page, not floating on it.
  • Quiet type. Labels are set in Archivo at medium weight (500): white on black needs no more weight to be read. Archivo is under the SIL Open Font License and covers Vietnamese. Apps that do not bundle it fall back to the system font.
  • Night is black. The dark modes put the canvas at pure black, so a phone on a desk at night shows almost no light.

Voice

Section titled “Voice”

Short, plain, and in the second person. Say what will happen and when it ends; never praise, never scold.

Write Not
Lock until 11:30 Start your amazing focus session!
Locked. Opens at 11:30. Oops! You can’t use this app right now.
End the session early Give up?
Delete this schedule Are you sure you want to delete?

Usage

Section titled “Usage”
  • One primary action per screen: it is the only black block on it.
  • Use the danger variant only to end something early or delete something, and say what.
  • Do not tint surfaces or text with a color of your own; the theme has none to offer.

Accessibility

Section titled “Accessibility”

Every declared pair meets its contrast requirement in all four modes: 4.5:1 for text in the standard modes, 7:1 in the high-contrast modes, and 3:1 for outlines and the focus ring.
