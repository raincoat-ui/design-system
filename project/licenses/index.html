License allowlist

Every dependency and bundled asset must carry one of these licenses. CI fails on anything else. All identifiers are OSI-approved SPDX licenses. Exceptions are individual, dated, and listed in licenses/curations.yml.

Scope Applies to Allowed licenses
ship Linked into published artifacts (npm packages, Android artifacts, the Swift package). 0BSD, Apache-2.0, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, ISC, MIT, Zlib
dev Build, test, and CI tooling that is never shipped. Includes everything in “ship”. 0BSD, Apache-2.0, BlueOak-1.0.0, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, EPL-2.0, ISC, MIT, MPL-2.0, Python-2.0, Zlib
fonts Font files, wherever they are committed. OFL-1.1
