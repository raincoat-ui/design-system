# RFC 0000: Short title

- Status: proposed <!-- proposed | accepted | declined | superseded by NNNN -->
- Kind: component <!-- component | token | theme-surface -->
- Proposed: YYYY-MM-DD

## Summary

Two or three sentences: what is proposed and who it is for.

## Problem

What cannot be done today, with a concrete example from an app.

## Proposal

For a component: anatomy, variants, states, and the tokens it uses.
For a token change: the names, tiers, values per mode, and whether themes may override them.

## Prior art

Compare against at least three systems from `docs/research/prior-art.md`, using the table there.

## Accessibility

Role and name, keyboard map, focus management, announcements, minimum target size, and how each
will be tested on web, iOS, and Android.

## Internationalization

Right-to-left behaviour, long and expanded text, Vietnamese diacritics, CJK line breaking.

## Platform notes

Anything that must differ between web, SwiftUI, and Compose, and why.

## Migration

For changes to existing tokens or components: what breaks, the deprecation path, and the codemod.

## Alternatives considered

What else was weighed and why it was not chosen.
